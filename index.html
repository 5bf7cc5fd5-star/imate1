Income & fund movement

How withdrawals work
1) Joining bonus (UGX 15,000) is credited on sign-up but locked until you buy club shares.
2) Buy club shares to unlock withdrawals and the joining bonus.
3) Submit amount + payout method (MTN / Airtel must match your registered phone, or USDT wallet).
4) 5% fee is deducted · funds move to Under Review with a system TxID (yellow).
5) System Approves / Disburses (green) or Rejects (red — amount returns to your Available balance).
6) Actual payout is sent on the method you chose after approval.
