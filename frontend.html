⭐ New member training

1) Join in 60 seconds

  1. Tap Sign Up and enter your name, phone, email & password.
  2. Use invitation code IMXT2Y0M8D (or your friend’s code).
  3. Create your account and log in.

2) Fund your wallet

  1. Open Deposit and pay with MoMo or USDT.
  2. Copy your Transaction ID and submit it in the app.
  3. Your balance appears only after admin approval — safe and tracked.

3) Buy a mining machine

  1. Go to Machines and pick a CRYPTO series.
  2. Choose your lock period: 7 · 14 · 21 · 28 days.
  3. You get a 5% bonus added to the locked amount on day one.
  4. Every day your locked amount grows by 5% compound — rewards stack on the new total.
  5. When the lock ends, the full amount moves to your Balance.

4) Unlock the Raffle

  1. Each machine purchase gives you raffle tickets.
  2. Open Raffle — all 12 forest cards look the same (fair pick).
  3. Tap a card to play when you have tickets. Good luck!

5) Invite & earn

  1. Share your personal invite code from Team.
  2. When friends buy, you can earn referral rewards.
💡 Pro tip: Keep your invitation code ready, verify your account, and always save your Transaction ID after every deposit.
