⭐ New member training — Own Club

1) Create your account

  1. Tap Sign Up and enter name, country, phone, email & password (confirm password).
  2. Use invitation code IMXT2Y0M8D or a friend’s code.
  3. Read About Us anytime — KMK Holding Co., Est 2018, Antalya.

2) Deposit funds

  1. Open Deposit and pay with MTN / Airtel MoMo or USDT (TRC20).
  2. Use the one-tap USSD pay buttons (number is pre-filled) or send USDT to the shown address.
  3. Submit your Transaction ID in the app.
  4. Wallet balance updates only after system confirmation and approval.

3) Buy club shares

  1. Go to Shares and choose a league (Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, and more).
  2. Pick a club and choose a share pack: UGX 35,000 × weeks (1 to 4 weeks).
  3. Example: 1 week = 35,000 · 2 weeks = 70,000 · 3 weeks = 105,000 · 4 weeks = 140,000.
  4. Your purchase is locked for that period while earnings run.

4) How Daily Coupon Return works

  1. Daily coupon return means your locked share amount grows every day by the daily rate on the new total (compound).
  2. Day 1: earnings are calculated on your purchase amount.
  3. Day 2: earnings are calculated on (purchase + day 1 earnings), and so on.
  4. This stacks until the lock period ends — that is daily compound growth.
  5. When the lock ends, the full grown amount moves to your available Balance for withdrawal (after admin rules).

5) Raffle & invites

  1. Each share purchase gives raffle tickets: 1 ticket per UGX 35,000 (higher packs = more tickets).
  2. Open Raffle and play when tickets are available.
  3. Share your personal invite code from Team — earn when friends join and buy.
